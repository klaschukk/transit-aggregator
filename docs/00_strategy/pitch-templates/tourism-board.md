# Tourism board / official body — formal outreach

**When to use:** national and city tourism organizations (serbia.travel,
tob.rs, novisad.travel, nis.travel, national parks), and government
portals (data.gov.rs, ministries). These targets need formal tone,
official identity on both sides, and usually a specific contact form
or info@ email.

**Goal:** add Prevozni to their "Useful links" / "Getting around" /
"Transport" page. They control the authority; we offer free,
up-to-date, multilingual data that they don't have to maintain.

**Success rate:** low (~5–10%) but each landed link is worth 10
low-DA blog links.

---

## Email template (EN)

> Subject: Free multilingual bus-schedule resource for [region] visitors
>
> Dear [Organization name],
>
> I'm [YOUR_NAME], developer of **Prevozni** (prevozni.com) — a free,
> non-commercial aggregator of bus and train schedules for Serbia, with
> city-transport coverage for Belgrade, Novi Sad, Niš and Kragujevac.
>
> Visitors to [REGION / CITY] regularly need this data (schedules,
> prices, carriers) before arriving, and your current
> [page URL they have, e.g. "Getting around" / "Transport info"] links
> only to individual carriers. A single aggregator page would let your
> audience compare options in seconds without clicking through four
> carrier websites — we cover [N] carriers, [N] routes, and support
> Serbian, English, and Russian.
>
> Would you consider adding a link to our main routes page,
> https://prevozni.com/rute/, in your "Getting around" / "Transport"
> section? There's no fee, no registration requirement, and the data
> stays current (we rescrape nightly).
>
> Happy to provide a 30-word description or a small banner if that
> helps. If you'd like reciprocal visibility, I'll gladly link back
> to your tourism page on our relevant city hubs
> (/gradski/beograd/, etc.).
>
> Thank you for considering it.
>
> Kind regards,
> [YOUR_NAME]
> prevozni.com · [YOUR_EMAIL] · [YOUR_PHONE_if_comfortable]

## Checklist before sending

- [ ] Named the correct specific page on their site (no "your website" vague)
- [ ] Offered a concrete deep link (/rute/, not homepage)
- [ ] Mentioned multilingual support (SR+EN+RU) — most carriers are SR only
- [ ] Included a reciprocal-link offer (even if they'll decline)
- [ ] Signed with a real name and direct email
- [ ] Subject line names the *benefit to them*, not "partnership request"
