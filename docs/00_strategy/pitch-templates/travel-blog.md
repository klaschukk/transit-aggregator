# Travel blog / content site — personalized pitch

**When to use:** blogger who published a Serbia/Balkans post in the
last 2 years, has a working comment section or contact form, and whose
post mentions buses without linking to a schedule resource. Needs
personalization of lines 1-2; the rest is template.

**Don't use on:** blogs with `DoFollow badge`, "write for us" pages
that want payment, or sites showing obvious link-sale patterns. Those
are traps.

**Success rate:** 15-25% for a well-personalized pitch.

---

## Email template

> Subject: Small suggestion for your [POST TITLE] piece
>
> Hey [FIRST_NAME],
>
> Enjoyed your [POST TITLE] — specifically the bit where you [QUOTE
> OR PARAPHRASE ONE SPECIFIC POINT from the post, ~10–15 words. This
> is the line that proves you actually read it]. It's genuinely one
> of the better takes on [SUBJECT] I've found recently.
>
> Quick thing you might want to know: the section on [e.g. "getting
> from Belgrade to Sarajevo by bus"] could use a real-time schedule
> link, since the individual carrier sites you linked are often out of
> date by a season or two. I run a free aggregator that pulls current
> schedules + prices for Serbia — no ads, no signup, multilingual
> (SR/EN/RU). If you want to drop in a single link, the exact route
> page is:
>
> https://prevozni.com/voznja/[from-slug]-do-[to-slug]/
>
> No expectation of a link exchange — I don't do those. Just a
> genuinely useful page for your readers who are about to book.
>
> Either way, thanks for writing the post. If you're ever doing a
> [COMPLIMENTARY OFFER — e.g. "Niš deep-dive"] and want data on
> carriers / routes / prices, I'm happy to pull whatever you need
> from the database.
>
> Cheers,
> [YOUR_NAME]
> prevozni.com

## Personalization variables

These must be filled from actually reading the post. Scripts that
templatize these fields = spam-filter material.

- `[POST TITLE]` — exact title of their article
- `[QUOTE OR PARAPHRASE]` — one specific sentence/claim they made.
  Paraphrase if the exact quote sounds creepy pasted in.
- `[SUBJECT]` — narrower than "travel", e.g. "Balkans slow travel",
  "Serbia on a budget", "Belgrade-Sofia overland"
- `[COMPLIMENTARY OFFER]` — a follow-up topic they might write that
  you could help with (data)

## What makes this work vs. generic outreach

1. Second sentence proves you read the post (or they assume bot & delete).
2. Line about "individual carrier sites are often out of date" makes
   their old post *less* good, which is the real reason to update it —
   they get to publish a micro-update and look current.
3. Explicit "no link exchange" — counterintuitively, bloggers trust
   this more than the usual "happy to discuss link exchange".
4. Genuine offer at the end (data / help on a future post) that only
   costs you 10 minutes but is rare in outreach.
