# Bus operator — reciprocal "useful links" ask (SR)

**When to use:** you're scraping the operator's schedule nightly (we
already do for Lasta, Niš-Ekspres, Srbija Voz, Arriva, Severtrans, SP
Lasta, Busplus, Naissus). They benefit from search traffic we send;
they can return a link on their `Korisni linkovi` / `Partneri` page.

**Don't use on:** operators whose Terms of Use forbid scraping. Check
/uslovi/ /terms/ before sending — if they prohibit scraping, do not
advertise that we scrape them.

**Success rate:** 10-20%. Operators move slowly but the link, when it
lands, sits on their homepage for years.

---

## Email template (SR — default)

> Subject: Red vožnje [operator] — korisan link za Vaš sajt
>
> Poštovani,
>
> Ja sam [YOUR_NAME], autor sajta **Prevozni** (prevozni.com) —
> besplatnog agregatora reda vožnje autobusa i vozova za Srbiju.
>
> Red vožnje [OPERATOR_NAME] je deo našeg pretraživača,
> i zahvaljujući tome korisnici koji traže "[primer pretrage, npr.
> 'autobus Beograd — Kraljevo']" dolaze na stranicu gde je Vaš
> prevoznik naveden sa imenom, cenom i vremenom polaska. Primer:
> https://prevozni.com/rute/beograd-kraljevo/ — prva tri polaska
> ovde su Vaši.
>
> Molba: ako imate sekciju "Korisni linkovi" ili "Partneri" na sajtu,
> da li biste dodali link ka prevozni.com ili konkretno ka stranici
> pretrage polazaka (https://prevozni.com/pretraga/)? Korisnicima
> olakšava kada traže više opcija u istom danu, a nama pomaže da
> povećamo dolazni saobraćaj, koji opet šalje više rezervacija u
> Vašem pravcu.
>
> Rado ću dodati Vaš logo i link na našu stranu prevoznika
> (https://prevozni.com/prevoznici/) ukoliko to bude zanimljivo.
>
> Hvala što razmotrite.
>
> Srdačan pozdrav,
> [YOUR_NAME]
> prevozni.com · [YOUR_EMAIL]

## Fields to fill

- `[OPERATOR_NAME]` — exact legal name as used on their site
- "primer pretrage" — a search term a user would realistically type,
  e.g. "autobus Beograd Niš" for Niš-Ekspres, "autobus Beograd Užice"
  for SP Lasta
- The example URL **must match a real route** on our site where the
  operator actually appears in the top 3 results. Check before
  sending — a fake example kills credibility.

## Checklist

- [ ] Verified scraping their site is TOS-allowed (no scraper clause)
- [ ] Picked a real route where they appear prominently
- [ ] Offered concrete reciprocal placement (our /prevoznici/ page)
- [ ] Sent from an email address that matches the domain (not gmail)
- [ ] Did NOT mention we're "open to buying links" or similar
- [ ] Only one follow-up, and stop for 12 months if silent after that
